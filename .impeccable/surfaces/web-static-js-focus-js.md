# Surface: focus group (`/focus-group`)

Mode: Operate. A tool page inside the 焦点 Focal world; inherits the tool grammar of /cutout, /erase and /heatmap.

Audience and job: designers, PMs and UX researchers who want a fast, honest rehearsal of a focus group before talking to real users. One research question in; optionally a screen flow (1–20 shots), an A/B pair of flows, or one screenshot with its HotGaze heatmap. Out: a transcript and a structured report they can download.

Constraints: discussion is generated by the DeepSeek API (the only tool that leaves the server; the page says so). Two steps by the user's choice: recruit participants and a guide, review and edit, then run. Bilingual; the session language follows the UI language. Nothing stored; results live in the browser. Sample session labelled with its provenance.

Memorable moment: the transcript writing itself, turn by turn, and the moderator revealing the heatmap to the group.

Unresolved: sample is simulated by Claude until a real DeepSeek export replaces it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An AI focus group as a working research tool, laid out as an interview transcript being written live, never a chatbot. Refuses chat bubbles, typing-dot theatrics and generated persona faces; keeps Focal's stage, panel and results grammar.

OWN-WORLD: Focal unchanged: white page, cool grey stage, near-black ink, one blue for the next step and the moderator's rule. Participants are monogram circles in Cool Surface Deep with Ink initials; moderator turns carry a 2px Focal Blue rule and act as section heads; status colours only for stance, severity and A/B data. The main column is a white hairline sheet, because its content is a document (brief, roster, transcript) and grey monograms vanish on a grey ground; screenshot trays inside it are the cool-grey drop targets. Hairlines, 16px containers, tabular readings.

STORY: The researcher states one question and its material, reviews and edits the recruited participants and discussion guide, watches the session unfold turn by turn with a stop control, then reads a structured report and downloads it as Markdown.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title plus one line. Left stage holds the current step: brief fields, the material segmented control (text, flow, A/B, heatmap) and the image tray, sample chip below. Right 320px panel: status, three-step progress (recruit, discuss, report), participant count, one primary action, recruit then start, which stays blue and busy while the session runs with a stop control beside it (blue only ever means the next step); fineprint on AI simulation and DeepSeek data handling.
